## Correções de gramática

### 1. Hometown

**Você disse:**

> He is visiting your born town.

**Melhor:**

> He is visiting **his hometown**.

Use **"his"** para se referir à cidade do viajante. **"Hometown"** é a palavra natural para cidade natal; "your" significa "sua", referindo-se à pessoa com quem você fala.

### 2. Get a ticket

**Você disse:**

> He take a ticket from York from the ticket machine.

**Melhor:**

> He **gets** a ticket **to** York from the ticket machine.

No presente simples, "he" exige o verbo com **"-s"**. Para obter uma passagem, use **"get a ticket"**; "to York" indica o destino. Esta correção melhora a frase, mas não confirma que essa ação ocorreu no vídeo.

### 3. Present perfect

**Você disse:**

> Yes, he's came from the Charcot.

**Melhor:**

> Yes, he **has just come** from the Harry Potter shop.

Pelo contexto da conversa, você parece estar se referindo à loja do Harry Potter. Depois de "has", use o particípio **"come"**, não o passado "came"; **"just"** indica que a ação acabou de acontecer.

### 4. Mind the gap

**Você disse:**

> It's a warning to you take careful about the empty space to the platform and to the train.

**Melhor:**

> It's a warning to **be careful** about the **gap between** the platform **and** the train.

A expressão correta é **"be careful"**, não "take careful". Use **"gap"** para o vão e **"between … and …"** para indicar o espaço entre duas coisas.

## Texto modelo

> **First**, he scans his ticket at the barriers. **Then**, he walks along the platform to his carriage. He **gets on** the train and **minds the gap**. **Finally**, he **gets off** the train in York. At the end, he says, **"We are home."**

Observe: presente simples na terceira pessoa (*scans, walks, gets*), sequência com **first / then / finally** e preposições (*along, to, in*).

## Expressões úteis

1. **First**, he scans his ticket at the barriers.
2. **Then**, he walks along the platform to his carriage.
3. He **gets on** the train and **minds the gap**.
4. He **gets off** the train in York.
5. London is only part of his trip because he is travelling home to York.
6. At the end, he says, **"We are home."**

## Gramática para revisar

- **Present simple** for describing events: he gets, he walks, he scans
- **Present perfect** for recent actions: he has just come from the shop → ver [Present Perfect — chegada recente](../present-perfect.md)
- **Possessive adjectives**: his hometown, your hometown
- **Prepositions**: to York, along the platform, between the platform and the train
- **Sequencing** with first, then, after that and finally
